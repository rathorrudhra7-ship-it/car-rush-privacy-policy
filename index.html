Car Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Car Rush is a mobile racing game developed by DOU Studios. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

2. Accounts and Login

Car Rush does not currently require users to create an account or log in.

3. Advertising

Car Rush uses Google AdMob to display banner and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers and ad interaction data according to applicable settings and policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

4. Game Progress

Game progress, coins and unlocked cars may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game may erase locally stored progress.

5. Children's Privacy

The game is subject to applicable age-related privacy requirements. Its intended audience and advertising settings should be configured accurately by the responsible publisher.

6. Data Security and Retention

Local game data remains on the device until deleted or the game is uninstalled. Advertising-related data is handled according to Google's applicable policies.
